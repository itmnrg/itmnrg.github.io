Shooting a roll

There are two ways to shoot

Use the film button at the top of the camera screen to switch between “One Roll” (the default) and “Develop Now”. Videos aren’t added to the roll and record as usual.

How do I shoot a roll?

Tap the film button → “Load a Roll” and choose a film while looking at the samples. A roll has 27 shots, and you can’t change the film until it’s finished. After each shot, swipe the wheel at the edge of the preview to advance the film (Camera Control and the volume buttons work too). You can turn off film advance in Settings → “Film Advance”.

Where are my shots?

Until you develop them, your shots stay only inside this app. They don’t go to the Photos app. Deleting the app also deletes undeveloped shots. Please develop your roll before you change devices or delete the app.

What happens when the roll is finished?

Press the shutter, or tap “View Negatives & Develop” on the film screen, to see your negatives. Tilt your iPhone as if holding it up to the light, or tap a frame, to see the real colors. “Develop” saves every frame to the Photos app, and then you can make a contact sheet (the whole roll on one image) or an Instagram strip. To develop before the roll is finished, tap “Rewind Here” on the film screen.

What does “Expires” mean?

Shots taken more than a week after loading the roll slowly change color as days pass, like real expired film (the effect is strongest after about three weeks). You can turn this off in Settings → “Expired Color”.

How do I rename or delete a developed roll?

On the film screen, under “Developed Rolls”, swipe left or touch and hold a roll. Deleting it doesn’t delete the photos saved in the Photos app.

How to

How do I add a film look to my photo? (Develop Now)

In “Develop Now”, the Develop screen opens after you take a photo. Choose a film, adjust the strength, grain and more, then tap “Save & Export”. You can also open photos and videos from your library with the button at the bottom left of the camera screen.

I want to save photos right away

Turn off Settings → “Open Develop After Capture” to save photos as-is without editing.

I want to save in Instagram or postcard sizes

On the “Save & Export” screen, choose a destination such as Instagram, X, Panorama or Postcard.

Troubleshooting

The camera or microphone doesn’t work

Go to the iPhone Settings app → NEGARO and turn on Camera and Microphone. Even without camera access, you can still develop photos from your library.

“Couldn’t save to Photos” appears

Go to the iPhone Settings app → NEGARO → Photos and choose “Add Photos Only” or higher.

Video export takes a long time

Export processes one frame at a time on your device. It keeps going if you switch to another app, but runs slower outside the App. If your device gets warm, export slows down automatically and keeps going.

My saved photos have no location

By default, location data is removed when saving to protect your privacy. You can change this in Settings → “Remove location when saving”.

Pro & purchases

What is Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase that you keep forever. It is not a subscription. It unlocks unlimited video length, 4K and ProRAW (supported devices only), the Pro film RAIN 200, AI film picks that match your photo and the light, special frames, papers and stamps, and more expired colors and contact sheet papers (black and kraft) for One Roll. Shooting a roll, developing it and making contact sheets are free.

I got a new device or reinstalled the App

Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy Pro, then tap Settings → “Restore Purchase”.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please request one from Apple’s Report a Problem page. The developer cannot process refunds.
