Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NEGARO (“the App”) handles your information.

SupportContactTerms of Use

1. Our approach

The App does not collect your information. Your photos and videos never leave your device. The App contains no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it doesn’t require an account.

2. Photos and videos

  • Capturing, the film look, portrait background effects, AI film picks, auto correction, skin smoothing and all other processing happen on your iPhone or iPad. AI features do not use any external server or service.
  • Your photos and videos are never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Edited photos and videos are added to the Photos app as new items. Your originals are never changed.
  • Location data is removed when saving by default (you can change this in Settings).
  • Video export keeps running on your device when you switch to another app. Your video is not sent anywhere during this time.

3. Permissions

The App asks for each permission only when you first use the related feature. You can still use other features if you decline.

PermissionPurpose
CameraTo take photos and videos
MicrophoneTo record sound with videos
Add to PhotosTo save edited photos and videos to the Photos app (add only; the App never reads your photo library)

When you choose photos or videos from your library, the App uses the standard iOS picker. Only the items you pick are opened, and the App never asks for access to your whole library. The App does not use location permission.

4. Sharing

Sharing to Instagram, X and other apps happens only when you choose a destination. The App hands the selected photo to that app or to the iOS share sheet and never sends it anywhere by itself. After sharing, the destination service’s own terms apply.

5. Purchases

Purchasing and restoring Pro is handled by Apple’s App Store. The developer never receives your payment information. The App only checks, on your device, the purchase status provided by Apple to see whether Pro is available. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles your information.

6. Information you send us

  • If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, any device details you include (app version, iOS version, device model, whether you have Pro), and any screenshots or images you attach.
  • The contact form on our support page only drafts an email and opens your mail app. The page itself does not send anything.
  • We use this information only to reply and to investigate problems.
  • We do not share it with third parties except as required by law.
  • We delete emails one year after the matter is resolved.

7. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page. Important changes will also be noted in the App’s update notes.
